Portals stories

Story packs for Portals, the Portal-like puzzle game for the Tanmatsu. Put them on your Konsool straight from here, over USB, or download them.

Pick a story Get the game

On your badge in three steps

  1. 1

    Plug in. Connect the Tanmatsu to this computer with USB.

  2. 2

    BadgeLink mode. On the badge's launcher, press the purple diamond: a USB icon shows in the top right.

  3. 3

    Put it on. Press Put it on my Tanmatsu under a story and pick Tanmatsu in the list. Then on the badge: Portals, Stories.

Loading the stories…

By hand

Unzip a story into /sd/portals/dlc/ on the badge's SD card, so that its pack.txt ends up in /sd/portals/dlc/<story>/, or copy it there with badgelink's fs upload from a terminal.

Portals 0.12.4 or later plays every story here; 0.12.5 or later plays each with its own music.

Write your own

A story is a folder of text files: a pack.txt that names it and lists its chambers, and a file for each chamber, drawn in letters. No compiler, no SDK.

name: After hours
about: The tests are over. The testing is not.
music: overtime bpm=110 key=D seed=42
chamber: overtime
chamber: momentum
chamber: rush-hour
…

How to write a story, with desk stories, mail, calendars and routes.

Portals is a fan game in honour of Valve's Portal. Portal, Aperture Science and GLaDOS are Valve's. Runs under Graceloader, built on SynthEngine 3D.

Made with Claude: Portals, its stories and this page were written with an AI, and played on a real badge.
