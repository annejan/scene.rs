Portals stories

Check my card

A story you put on the badge is not in Portals' Stories list? This reads the Tanmatsu's SD card over USB and says why: a story copied in part, unzipped a folder too deep, still a zip, or somewhere the game does not look. Then it puts it right, if you ask. Reading changes nothing on the card.

Check it

  1. 1

    Plug in. Connect the Tanmatsu to this computer with USB.

  2. 2

    BadgeLink mode. On the badge's launcher, press the purple diamond: a USB icon shows in the top right.

  3. 3

    Check. Press the button and pick Tanmatsu in the list.

      Also in dlc/

        Where a story goes

        Each story is a folder in /sd/portals/dlc/, with its pack.txt straight in it: /sd/portals/dlc/hackfest/pack.txt. Not the zip, and not a folder in a folder: some computers unzip hackfest.zip into hackfest/hackfest/. Portals 0.13.3 and later find a story a folder too deep, and list a folder they cannot play under Stories, with why.

        Portals is a fan game in honour of Valve's Portal. Portal, Aperture Science and GLaDOS are Valve's.
